👋 You're invited to Habit Assistant!

A private Telegram bot for logging little daily habits — water, stretching, a diary entry, or anything else — and gently keeping each other on track. It understands plain English or Thai. No app to install.

⏱ Getting started (20 seconds)
1️⃣ Open this chat and send /start
2️⃣ Wait for approval — you'll get a message the moment you're in, usually quick
3️⃣ You're set! Every log, streak, and reminder from here is yours alone

✨ Try these 3 things
💧 Log something — just type "500ml" or "10 min stretch"
❓ Send /help to see everything the bot can do
📋 Send /habits to see what's tracked and how today's going

Made a mistake? Every confirmation has its own ↩️ Undo button — no harm done.

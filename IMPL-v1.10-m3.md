## How it works

`core/pause.py`'s shared-surface fail-open wrappers (`is_paused_safe`, `active_pauses_safe` — already built and tested in `IMPL-v1.10-shared.md`) each catch any exception from their underlying `pause.is_paused`/`db.active_pauses` call, log it, and return the fail-open answer (`False` / `[]`) instead of propagating. The fix at all 4 of my owned sites is mechanical: swap the raw call for its `_safe` counterpart at the exact same call site, same arguments, no other logic touched. Since none of the 4 functions (`build_checkin_message`, `build_nudge_message`, `compute_daily_summary`, `compute_weekly_stats`) wrap their own pause-read in a try/except of their own, a raise there previously propagated all the way out to whatever loop called them — for `run_due_checkins`, `daily_summary_job`, and `weekly_review_job` that loop has **no** per-user try/except at all, so one user's pauses-table hiccup used to abort the entire tick/job for every user after them in iteration order. Routing through the `_safe` helpers means the exception is now swallowed one level in, so the outer loop never even sees it.
